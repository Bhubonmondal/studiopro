# Welcome to Studio Pro!

Hey there! I built Studio Pro because I wanted a powerful, completely free video recording and presentation tool that runs right inside your web browser. You can check it out live at bhubanmondal.me/studiopro.

I made sure to design it so that anyone can use it without needing to be a computer expert. It feels like a professional video studio, but it is super easy to understand!

## Cool Things It Can Do

I packed a lot of fun and useful features into this tool to make your videos look great:

-   **Record Video and Audio**: You can easily connect your webcam and microphone to record yourself.
    
-   **Share Your Screen**: You can record your computer screen to show people what you are working on.
    
-   **Whiteboard Drawing**: I built a digital whiteboard where you can use pens, highlighters, and erasers to draw while you talk.
    
-   **Present PDFs**: You can upload PDF documents or pictures and present them to your viewers like a slideshow.
    
-   **AI Background Blur**: Do not want people to see your messy room? You can blur your background or replace it with a custom picture.
    
-   **Teleprompter**: I added a floating text box that scrolls your script automatically so you never forget what to say.
    
-   **Reels Mode**: If you want to make tall videos for mobile phones, you can switch the camera to a vertical mode.
    
-   **Camera Tweaks**: You can easily zoom in, move the camera left or right, and fix the brightness or contrast.
    

## How to use it

You do not need to install any heavy, complicated software.

1.  Visit the live site at bhubanmondal.me/studiopro
    
2.  Give your browser permission to use your camera and microphone when it asks.
    
3.  Pick your tools from the left and right sidebars, hit the red "Start Recording" button, and have fun!
